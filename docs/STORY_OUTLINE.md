# Story Outline, draft 1

Built on The God of Cookery's structure (see [`GOD_OF_COOKERY_STUDY.md`](GOD_OF_COOKERY_STUDY.md)), with our characters, setting and goal.

> **Fall → kindness from below → street comeback → pilgrimage to a humble master → loss → the simple dish → rigged contest → the truth wins → "everyone has one".**

**Approved:** a public failure on day 1 · our "Turkey" is a street vendor and grandpa's superfan · a livestream judge paid by Wang, overruled by the people you helped.

## Prologue: The fake god (Day 0, Ireland)

- A grey Dublin office. You type prompts for business decks. **This is the tutorial: the player types.**
- Your prompts drift: "Q3 synergy deck" → "Q3 deck, noodle-themed" → "show me cross-bridge rice noodles" → "how are they made".
- You open a photo from your trip to Kunming years ago: you, grinning, with Grandpa Zhao and three empty bowls.
- You search the shop… **CLOSED.** ("WHAT?!?!")
- You tell your boss it's an emergency. 22 days of annual leave. You book the flight.
- On the plane you ask the AI for "the perfect cross-bridge noodle recipe". It gives you one, with confidence. **You believe it.**

---

## Act 1: The fall (Days 1–3)

### Day 1: Arrival and public failure *(film: the fall)*
- Kunming in spring. The shutters on Old Zhao's are down. Across the street, Wang's neon chain is opening, with drums, dancers and a livestream.
- Grandma finds you ("the tall foreigner who ate three bowls!"). Grandpa is gone. "**Wang stole his recipe.**"
- You announce you'll fix this. You have **the AI's perfect recipe**. You cook it, in public, on the pavement, while Wang's livestream is running.
- **It's a disaster.** Grandma's face goes through five emotions. Wang laughs into the camera. The clip goes viral: *"Foreigner insults cross-bridge noodles."*
- Night. You're sitting on the kerb, broke, with your phone at 3%.

### Day 1, night: Kindness from below *(film: Turkey's pork rice)*
- **Little Chilli** stomps over from her stall, shouting at you for embarrassing grandpa's name, and shoves a bowl into your hands.
- It's **plain**: simple broth, noodles, a few chives. Nothing special. You eat it in silence, and it's wonderful.
- She mutters that grandpa used to feed her this when she was a kid with nowhere to go.
- **🔑 Hidden key:** this bowl holds grandpa's core method. First-time players think it's "just nice". At the end it's the answer.
- She asks you to finish painting the sign for her stall. You say "sure, later" and forget. *(Film: the heart drawing.)*

### Days 2–3: The job
- You're broke. First paycheck only comes when you're back at work. **Sign up for Little Yellow Duck.** You get your duck-bill helmet.
- Tutorial deliveries around grandpa's street. Meet the translator app, Xiao Lan (who overtakes you, waving), and the mahjong aunties.
- **Aunties gag #1:** your first delivery route goes straight through the square-dance plaza. You're chased out by eighteen aunties with fans.
- First evening at grandma's: **Bowl Lab begins.** She cooks whatever you bring home, however awful.

---

## Act 2a: The street (Days 4–10)

### Free play: deliver, taste, scheme, befriend
- Kunming zones open up: Green Lake (the last gulls of the season), old town, Dounan flower market, Guandu, the Western Hills.
- **Plankton schemes** on restaurants' secret recipes. Lots of failure, lots of clues.
- **The Plain-Broth Customer** appears in the orders: one plain bowl, a different town each day.
- The neighbourhood slowly comes around on you, the weird foreigner.

### The feud *(film: beef balls vs. shrimp)*
- Little Chilli and Uncle Spud fight daily over one metre of pavement. Their customers are drifting off to Wang's chain anyway.
- Around **day 6–10** (whenever you've befriended both): the player, the Irishman, says the obvious thing:

  **"Why don't you just put the potato *in* the erkuai?"**

- **Combination puzzle** in the Bowl Lab (teaches the system): erkuai + fried potato + the right sauce from each stall.
- The result is a hit, named by a bad translation (e.g. **"Explosive Bait Potato"**, since 饵块 → "bait block"). Queues down the alley.
- **First real money. First real respect. The first thing you ever made yourself.**
- The rivals share the pavement. Potatoes finally earn the Irishman some honour.

---

## Midpoint: The betrayal (Days 10–11) *(film: the business partner)*
- **The landlord has sold grandpa's shop lease to Wang.**
- Wang announces the **Supreme Cross-Bridge Noodle Championship** on **Day 22**, livestreamed by **Sister Sparkle**. Prize: **the old Zhao shop.**
- Now there's a concrete goal: **win the shop back on the last day of your leave.**
- Wang offers to buy your Explosive Bait Potato and hire you. Turning him down is a choice; accepting leads straight to the bad ending.

---

## Act 2b: The pilgrimage (Days 12–17)

### Qingming (清明, ~Day 13) *(our emotional beat)*
- The day for remembering family. Grandma takes you along to sweep her parents' graves.
- On the way home she tells you how she and grandpa met: she was sick, in a village across the water, and every day a young man carried soup to her **across the bridge**. He kept it hot with a layer of oil. *(The legend, but it's theirs.)*
- **Clue:** "He never cooked for customers. He cooked for *a* person. Every bowl."

### Mengzi: The academy that isn't *(film: Chinese Cooking Academy → Shaolin kitchen)*
- High-speed train to **Mengzi**, birthplace of cross-bridge noodles. You expect a great **Cross-Bridge Noodle Academy**.
- You find a tacky theme park… **franchised by Wang.** (Of course.)
- Lost and miserable, you deliver an order to an old woman by a lake with a stone bridge: **the Bridgekeeper**. She cooks one bowl a day.
- **Training (a montage that's also gameplay):** skim broth for hours, pluck, slice meat thin enough to read through, and carry a full bowl **across her bridge without spilling a drop**, again and again. Every delivery skill you've built gets tested.
- **Aunties gag #2:** a touring square-dance group in Mengzi. Same fans. Same result.
- She knew grandpa when he was young. She won't tell you the recipe: "**Your hands already know it.**"

### The loss *(film: Turkey takes the bullet)*
- Back in Kunming, **around Day 15–16**, one of your Plankton schemes against Wang's chain goes wrong (or you're about to be caught).
- **Little Chilli takes the blame** to protect you. Wang's lawyers and the city inspectors close her stall. Her pavement is empty. She's gone, and her phone is off.
- It's your fault. You stop scheming.
- **Grief changes the game:** the music drops and the city goes quiet for a day. Grandma's Bowl Lab feedback starts mentioning "*who is this for?*"

## Act 3: The bowl (Days 18–22)

### Subtraction (Days 18–20)
- The Bowl Lab flips: you stop adding and start **taking away**. Each removal gets closer. *(The simplicity lesson, through play.)*
- You remember **Little Chilli's day-1 bowl**. It was the answer all along. *(Speedrunners who knew could have used it on day 2.)*

### Finding grandpa (Day ~20)
- The Plain-Broth Customer's orders form a trail. You deliver to the last address and find **grandpa**, eating alone at a roadside stall.
- He's lost: he started piling toppings on his bowl to compete with Wang, it got worse, he was ashamed, and he left.
- He won't come back. "*Simple isn't enough any more.*"
- You leave him a bowl, carried there hot. He doesn't touch it while you watch.

### Day 22: The Championship *(film: the final)*
- The square in front of grandpa's shop. Neon, drones, Sister Sparkle's livestream with a million viewers.
- **Wang's bowl:** 48 toppings, gold leaf, a sparkler, served on a revolving platform.
- **Your bowl:** broth, oil, noodles, a few slices, a few chives. Little Chilli's bowl, made properly.
- **The tasting:** Sister Sparkle tastes yours. Massive, over-the-top reaction: tears, a flashback to her own grandmother, slow motion, light beams (the **"Food Reveal"**). Then she looks at her sponsor contract and announces: **"The winner is… Wang."** *(Rigged.)*

### The crossing *(our version of "heaven steps in")*
- You don't argue. You pick up a fresh bowl, put the oil layer on top, and walk out with it.
- **Final delivery.** Across the city, through the crowd, to **the stone bridge** where grandpa is sitting. The livestream follows you. Keep it hot. Don't spill. Everything you've learned.
- **Aunties gag #3, now a payoff:** Wang's people try to block you, and the square-dance aunties form a wall and dance them out of the way.
- **Everyone you helped is there**, lining the bridge. (The crowd's size depends on how many NPC stories you finished.)
- Grandpa tastes it. Silence. Then he cries. *"This is my bowl. …No. It's yours."*
- The livestream comments flood in. The crowd walks grandpa back to his shop. Sister Sparkle, live, tears up her contract.
- Grandpa: **"There's no secret recipe. Or rather, everyone has one. You just have to cook it for someone."**

### Epilogue
- Old Zhao's reopens. Wang's chain is quietly empty.
- A new stall on the corner: **Little Chilli is back.** (She'd gone to her home village to cool off.) You finally hand her **the painted sign** you promised on Day 1.
- Ending choice: fly back to the laptop… or stay one more spring.

| **Chain Store** | Never solved, or you took Wang's job. Wang's sign hangs over grandpa's door. You can keep riding forever. |

---

## Free play vs. fixed beats

Fixed (happen on set days, Majora's Mask style): **Day 1 failure, Day 10–11 midpoint, Qingming, Day 15–16 loss, Day 22 championship.**

Flexible (trigger when conditions are met; the player chooses when): the feud combination, the Mengzi trip, the Plankton schemes, finding grandpa (as early as you can track him), every NPC story.

That keeps the story's shape for new players and leaves routes open for speedrunners.
