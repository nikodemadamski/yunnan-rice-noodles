# The opening: shot list and the rules behind it

Goal: in about 40 seconds, make the player *feel* why a man would quit his
desk for a bowl of noodles. Show the love first; take it away second.

## Rules we cut by
- **Show, don't tell.** Almost no on-screen text: two quiet lower-third captions and four short lines of dialogue.
- **Emotion = cut closer.** Big moments go to a close-up or a slow push-in. A wide shot follows to breathe.
- **Shot / reverse-shot, 180° rule.** In a dialogue, both cameras stay on one side of the line between the speakers.
- **Comedy lives in the cut.** Hard cut to the stamp, hit-zoom, then a *beat of silence* before "WHAT?!". The boss's pause before "…Go." is the joke.
- **Cut on action** (the stamp landing, him bolting) and **match cut** (bowl in Kunming → the same bowl on his monitor).
- **Pace.** Shots are 1.5–3 s; reactions are held a little longer than feels comfortable.
- **Lens maths.** Characters are ~3 units tall with ~1.9-unit heads. Distance = half frame height ÷ tan(fov/2): MCU ≈ 4.5 at 32°, CU ≈ 3.2, ECU ≈ 3 at 24–27°. When dialogue shows, aim below the face so it sits in the upper third, above the box.
- **Grade tells the time.** Memory: warm, golden, music. Dublin: desaturated, cool vignette, rain, no music. Inserts of the photo are in full colour, so the warmth punches through the grey.
