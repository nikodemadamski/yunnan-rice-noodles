# Scene: The SIM Card (name entry + haggling)

Replaces "type your name for the boss". 2–4 minutes, playable.

## Why this scene
- **True to life:** foreigners must buy a SIM with **real-name registration**: passport details tied to the number, often a photo/face scan. Without a Chinese number you can't use WeChat, ride-hailing or food delivery, which sets up the Little Yellow Duck job later.
- **Built-in haggling:** official plan prices are fixed, but **phone numbers** aren't. Numbers full of 8s (八 bā ≈ 發 fā, "prosper") cost more; numbers with 4s (四 sì ≈ 死 sǐ, "death") are cheap and pile up unsold.
- **The name entry is a joke the player is in on:** a foreign name copied onto a Chinese form gets mangled.

## Cast
- **Auntie Duan (段阿姨):** runs a cramped phone stall in old Kunming. Lucky cat, thermos of Pu'er, a giant calculator she calls **"the Judge"**. Kunming dialect: 板扎 *bǎnzhā* ("great"), 老表 *lǎobiǎo* ("buddy").
- **Xiao Li:** her nephew, translating badly through a phone app.

## Beats
1. **Arrival (20 s):** the airport counter has a long queue and a ¥200 sign. A flyer: "Auntie Duan – SIM – cheap – English (a bit)".
2. **Plan pick (20 s):** fixed menu: ¥88 (10 GB), ¥128 (20 GB), ¥198 (unlimited "for TikTok"). "Price is price, 老表."
3. **Number pick + haggle (90 s):** see below.
4. **Real-name form = name entry (60 s):** see below.
5. **Face scan + payoff (20 s):** the photo includes the cat; the first text message arrives… "Now you need WeChat."

## Haggling (number fee only)
| Number | Ask | Floor |
|---|---|---|
| 138-8888-1688 "Emperor" | ¥888 | ¥288 |
| 139-2468-0517 ordinary | ¥50 | ¥20 |
| 187-4444-1414 cursed | ¥0 + free sunflower seeds, blessed with a joss stick | — |

- Type an offer into **the Judge**; she slides it back with a counter.
- **Patience: 5 teacups** that cool as you push.
  - 太贵了 / mild disinterest: free.
  - Lowball (< 30% of ask): −2.
  - Praise her cat: +1.
  - Fake walk-away: she calls you back if you're within 25% of her floor; otherwise you sheepishly return.
- **Tells (Moonlighter-style 4 moods):** she sips tea when near her floor; the cat's paw slows when you overreach.
- **"Just" bonus (Recettear):** offer within ¥5 of her floor in one go → "You're Kunming already!" + a free phone case.
- **Cosy failure:** patience runs out → she sighs, sells the ordinary number at full price, adds a free tea egg "because you're too thin". Every branch ends with a working SIM.
- **Unlocks:** Emperor number → fridge magnet; cursed number → "Brave Foreigner" sticker; Auntie Duan friendship flag.

## Name entry (Papers, Please-style form)
- Passport on the left (the real text input), form on the right.
- Auntie Duan copies the name **mangled**, with generic rules so any name works:
  - surname and given name swapped;
  - accents (fadas) and apostrophes stripped ("System no like ' ");
  - read aloud as pinyin syllables (e.g. *Siobhán O'Brien* → "OBRIEN SIOBHAN", said as Xī-ào-bān 西奥班).
- Tap the wrong fields to correct them, two fixes allowed. The third try gives a compromise and a hand-written nickname (e.g. 小西 "Little Xi") that NPCs use later. The passport keeps the exact spelling as the save name.

## Sample dialogue
1. 段：来来来，老表！手机卡要吗？ (Come, come, buddy! Need a SIM card?)
2. Player: How much for the number with all the eights?
3. 段：八百八十八。很吉利！ (888 yuan. Very lucky!)
4. Player: 太贵了！ (Too expensive!)
5. 段：哎哟…你是爱尔兰人？爱尔兰也有八吗？ (Aiyo… you're Irish? Do you have eights in Ireland?)
6. Player: 最低多少？ (What's your lowest?)
7. 段 *(sips tea, types 588 on the Judge)*: 计算器不会说谎。 (The calculator does not lie.)
8. Player: Fine. What about this one, with all the fours?
9. 段：四四四四…送你！还送瓜子。 (Four-four-four-four… it's yours, free! Sunflower seeds too.)
10. Xiao Li *(app voice)*: "Auntie says: your death number is very cheap and very fresh."
11. 段 *(writing the form)*: 你的名字…西—奥—班？ (Your name… Xi–ao–ban?)
12. 段：板扎！小西。好记。 (Great! Little Xi. Easy to remember.)
